# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

MIVA Open University assessors, peers, and potential collaborators reviewing Ubong George's academic profile, projects, and student planning workflow.

## Product Purpose

Present Ubong's cybersecurity studies, professional background, practical projects, and contact details while demonstrating responsive semantic web development and an interactive academic planner.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, quietly confident. The experience should feel welcoming and capable, with direct copy and useful interactions.

## Anti-references

Avoid gimmicky motion, decorative dashboards, generic template card grids, low-contrast text, and inflated claims. Keep the student profile connected to real work from the supplied portfolio.

## Design Principles

- Show real work and real background details.
- Keep the planner useful without turning the portfolio into an admin dashboard.
- Make every action understandable through its label and immediate feedback.
- Let the same content remain easy to use on a phone, with keyboard and reduced-motion support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, labeled form controls, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences. Use native video controls and provide a text description of the silent project montage.
